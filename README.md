Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479).

`main.ts` (Node 22, built-ins only) writes a two-line entry to `/output/entry.txt`: line 1 explains a toaster to a 10-year-old with one concrete analogy (a tiny lift for bread); line 2 honestly says what the analogy maps and what it leaves out.
